About Ranganayaki Builders

In 2008, Ranganayaki Builders was formed by Mr.L.Suresh with the blessing of his reputed mother Mrs.Ranganayaki, the former Counseller of Perungudi Panchayat. She was the driving force for all the values that the company posseses today. A highly Quality Consious Construction Company with Ethical Values aware of the market demand and supply, Ranganayaki Builders started with a strong foundation and steady growth commencing with construction projects of half a million and has grown today to executing projects over 5 crores.The company is consious of its responsibilies to the society by way of Health, Safety and Environment maintaining a highly satisfied and motivated work force. Mr.L.Suresh is a living example himself of construction values preserving stringent time schedules and completion on time conforming to the CMDA and Corporation normes and stipulation.
